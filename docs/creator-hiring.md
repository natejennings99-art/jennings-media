## 4. Interview questions (the "Contacted" call, about 15 minutes)

1. **Walk me through a recent shoot you're proud of: the brief, how you lit
   or framed it, and what you delivered.**
   *Listen for:* specifics (lens, light, shot list), not adjectives. They
   should be able to explain the choices they made.
2. **For a 2,500 sq ft listing, how long are you on site, and what's your shot
   list?**
   *Listen for:* about 60–90 minutes, 25–40 frames, straight verticals,
   window pulls or flash, and a clear order.
3. **What do you shoot on, and what's your backup if a body or drone fails on
   the day?**
   *Listen for:* a second body or a plan B. For drone work: a Part 107
   certificate number and their airspace checks (Tampa has controlled
   airspace near TPA, MacDill and Albert Whitted).
4. **Show me a vertical reel you cut. Why did you open on that shot?**
   *Listen for:* they know the first 1–3 seconds decide whether people keep
   watching, they cut for sound-off viewing, and the captions are safe from
   the app's buttons.
5. **What's your turnaround for 30 edited photos, or a 30-second reel?**
   *Listen for:* 24–48 hours for photos and 2–3 days for reels, said as a
   promise they keep.
6. **Tell me about a shoot that went wrong. What happened, and what did you
   tell the client?**
   *Listen for:* they owned it and told the client early. They don't blame
   the client.
7. **What's your rate for a half-day and a full day, and what's included
   (editing, travel, drone)?**
   *Listen for:* a clear number and a clear scope. Write it down, because it
   goes on their file in Admin → Creators.
8. **Are you set up as a business (W-9, your own liability or equipment
   insurance), and which days can you usually do?**
   *Listen for:* a W-9 is a must. Insurance is a plus, but it's required for
   drone work on client properties. Get specific days, not "flexible".

**Next step:** if the answers are clear and the portfolio is consistent, book
the paid trial shoot and move them to *Trial shoot* in Admin → Creators. If
not, move them to *No* and send a short, polite reply.

## 5. Test-shoot brief (one page, send before the trial)

**Jennings Media: paid trial shoot**

**What it is:** one real job, paid at the rate you quoted
(`$____`). It shows us how you work, start to finish, so we can send you
more.

**The job**
- Type: ☐ Real estate photo ☐ Reel ☐ Event
- Address / venue: ______________________
- Date and arrival time: ______________ (arrive 10 minutes early)
- On-site contact: ______________________
- Access: ______________________ (lockbox, front desk, host)

**Deliverables**
- *Real estate:* 25–35 edited photos, 3:2, about 2,400 px on the long edge,
  verticals straight, windows balanced, no people or personal items in shot.
  Optional: 5 drone stills (only if Part 107 and the airspace is clear).
- *Reel:* one 20–30 second vertical video (1080×1920), hook in the first two
  seconds, captions inside the safe area, no copyrighted music. Also send 10
  raw clips.
- *Event:* 40–60 edited photos, candid-first, with key moments covered (see
  the shot list), plus one 15-second vertical recap.

**Shot list:** attached, or agreed by text the day before.

**Delivery:** upload to the shared folder we send you, named
`YYYY-MM-DD_address-or-client_yourname`, within **48 hours** (photos) or **72
hours** (reels). Text Nate at (571) 317-3211 when it's in.

**On site**
- Introduce yourself as shooting for Jennings Media.
- Don't move or photograph anything the client hasn't OK'd: mail, family
  photos, screens, documents.
- Don't post any of this job's work anywhere until we say it's cleared with
  the client.

**How we judge it:** technical quality (sharp, level, exposed), whether you
followed the brief and shot list, whether you were on time and how you dealt
with the client, and whether it was delivered on time to spec.

**Pay:** your quoted rate, paid on delivery by the method and date we agree
before the shoot. Send your W-9 before the shoot.

**After:** we'll call within a few days. If it's a fit, you're on the list for
shoots in your area. If it isn't, we'll tell you why.
