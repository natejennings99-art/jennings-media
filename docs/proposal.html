How to use (this box does not print)

  • Click any yellow field and type. Fields turn white once filled.
  • Add price lines from the list below, or type your own. The total adds itself up.
  • When done: File → Print → Save as PDF. Turn off "Headers and footers" in the print dialog.
  • Your typing is not saved. Print to PDF before you close the tab.
